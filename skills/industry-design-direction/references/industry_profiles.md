# Industry design profiles

One row per product/industry type: the aesthetic direction that fits it, the
page structure that converts for it, and the constraint that most often gets
missed. Palettes live in `color_palettes.md` (same product-type key), font
pairings in `typography_pairings.md`, page structures in `landing_patterns.md`.

Match on the product type *or* its keywords — the keyword column exists because
users describe their product in their own words ("clinic booking site"), not in
these labels ("Healthcare App").

## SaaS (General)

- **Matches**: app, b2b, cloud, general, saas, software, subscription
- **Primary style**: Glassmorphism + Flat Design
- **Also works**: Soft UI Evolution, Minimalism
- **Page structure**: Hero + Features + CTA
- **If it has a dashboard**: Data-Dense + Real-Time Monitoring
- **Palette direction**: Trust blue + accent contrast
- **Most-missed constraint**: Balance modern feel with clarity. Focus on CTAs.

## Micro SaaS

- **Matches**: app, b2b, cloud, indie, micro, micro-saas, niche, saas, small, software, solo, subscription
- **Primary style**: Flat Design + Vibrant & Block
- **Also works**: Motion-Driven, Micro-interactions
- **Page structure**: Minimal & Direct + Demo
- **If it has a dashboard**: Executive Dashboard
- **Palette direction**: Vibrant primary + white space
- **Most-missed constraint**: Keep simple, show product quickly. Speed is key.
